§2

Educational use

Our lessons simplify complex topics in philosophy, psychology and media studies for learners. Scholars sometimes disagree, and curricula differ between schools and countries. For coursework and exams, follow your textbooks, exam board guidance and your teachers.

Everything here is general study guidance, not a syllabus and not an official source. If a teacher, tutor or board tells you something different, follow them. Our material is meant to sit alongside your course, to explain techniques that are rarely taught directly, and to save you time; it is never a substitute for the material you are actually being assessed on.

§3

Interactive tools

The right-triangle explorer and quadratic grapher offer general, illustrative guidance only. Marking schemes, rounding rules, resit caps and referencing conventions differ between institutions, so your own course handbook and your tutor are the authority on all of them.

The tools run entirely in your browser, which means your entries are never sent to us and nothing is stored on our side. They work from typical values and ordinary assumptions, so they give you a sensible starting point rather than a precise answer for your own situation. Treat the results as a guide, check them against the instructions supplied with whatever you are actually using, and use your own judgement. If a tool gives you a result that looks wrong, please tell us, because that is usually how we find a mistake.

Accuracy

We work hard to keep lessons accurate and up to date, but errors can occur. If you spot one, please let us know so we can correct it.

We take care over what we publish and we correct mistakes quickly when they are pointed out, but we cannot promise that every page is complete or current at the moment you read it. Standards change, courses are rewritten and recommendations move on. The date at the top of each guide tells you when it was last reviewed. Where something matters to your result, confirm it against your own course material or examination board before you rely on it.
